How to Arrive in San Pedro

How to Arrive in San Pedro

USGAR Hotels is located in the historic San Pedro neighborhood of Cusco. We provide private airport pickups to ensure a smooth and safe arrival.

Private Airport Pickup

Velasco Astete Airport is ~20 minutes away by car. We coordinate your private driver directly.

Altitude Sickness (Soroche)

Cusco is at 3,400m altitude. We offer 24/7 complimentary oxygen concentrators and warm coca tea to ensure your comfort.

Continuous bilingual assistance for late check-in, complimentary luggage storage, and Machu Picchu excursion arrangements.

Arrival FAQs

Arrival FAQs

Can I store my luggage before check-in or after check-out?

Yes! We offer 100% free, secure luggage storage while you explore Cusco or travel to Machu Picchu.

Is medical oxygen for altitude sickness included free of charge?

Yes, complimentary oxygen concentrators and coca tea infusions are available for guests 24/7 at no extra cost.

How do I arrange an airport pickup?

You can request it when submitting this form or select it during direct online room booking.
